# Imperial Platinum — Wild Pokémon

> Generated by `tools/design/gen_encounters.py` from Renegade Platinum v1.3.0's `WildPokemon.txt` and `data/species.csv`. Do not hand-edit; change the generator (or add overrides) and re-run.

## General notes

- Species marked **★** are new to Imperial Platinum (Gen V–IX). Everything else is a Renegade Platinum encounter.
- Levels are unchanged from Renegade Platinum. Fishing levels: Old Rod Lv. 10, Good Rod Lv. 25, Super Rod Lv. 50.
- Grass tables use the Gen IV slot rates 20/20/10/10/10/10/5/5/4/4/1/1. Slots 3 and 4 (the two 10% slots used by time of day) may change at Morning/Day/Night.
- Surf and each rod use the Gen IV slot rates 60/30/5/4/1.
- Poké Radar and Honey Tree encounters are unchanged from Renegade Platinum.
- Legendary, Mythical, Ultra Beast and Paradox Pokémon are not wild encounters; see `PostgameLegendaries.md`.
- Gen V–IX starters are Pokémon Center gifts, not wild encounters; see `GiftPokemon_Imperial.md`.
- This is a **design draft**: the placements come from a type/habitat/level heuristic and should be playtested and hand-tuned.

Added regular species placed directly in the wild: **389**. Not directly placed (obtain by evolution / breeding / events): **8** — listed at the bottom.
