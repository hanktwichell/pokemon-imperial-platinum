# Sovereign Platinum — Item Changes

Renegade Platinum's item changes and item locations are unchanged unless listed here
(`RenegadePlatinumV130/Documentation/ItemChanges.txt`).

In the game so far (E10 parts 2–11):
- Renegade's six TM moves (Scald, Wild Charge, Bug Buzz, Hyper Voice, Dazzling Gleam, Hurricane), TMs as 99 of each
  and unsellable, and the Poké/Great/Ultra Ball prices.
- Renegade's overworld item balls.
- Renegade's hidden items are Platinum's.
- Renegade's TM gifts from Fantina (TM30), Bertha (TM26), Floaroma's swordsman (TM40), the Underground Man house
  (fossils, TM28, TM80), Flint (TM50), Aaron (TM81) and Mira (TM85), and the rest of Renegade's TM and HM gifts
  (`EngineChangelog.md`, E10 parts 4–7), HM04 from Riley after Iron Island's tag battle, and the Iron Plate from
  Iron Island's Steel-type Master. Rowan gives the Poké Radar and 10 Repels in Sandgem Town.
- Renegade's other 15 Type Masters, each giving their Plate for a win with one Pokémon of their type (E10 part 20):
  Insect (Route 202), Stone (Route 207), Spooky (Lost Tower 1F), Fist (Veilstone south-west house), Toxic (Pastoria
  observatory gate 2F), Earth (Oreburgh Gate 1F), Meadow (Floaroma Meadow), Mind (Solaceon Ruins), Zap (Vista
  Lighthouse), Icicle (Snowpoint City), Splash (Route 219), Draco (Celestic Town cave), Flame (Route 226), Dread
  (Sendoff Spring), Sky (Pokémon League).
- The Snowpoint City evolution-item seller: 34 items at ₽10,000 each.
- No TMs in the shops: Veilstone 3F sells evolution stones and the Game Corner held items, as in Renegade.
- Renegade's NPC item gifts (part 9): the Electirizer and Magmarizer (Hotel Grand Lake), the Reaper Cloth (Lost
  Tower 1F), the Dragon Scale (a battle in the Veilstone Pokémon Center), the Dubious Disc (Veilstone northeast house),
  the Choice Band (Route 214), the Super Rod (Snowpoint's east house, or the Fight Area, whichever comes first) and the
  Item Fanatic's species items in Floaroma.
- The Route 208 Rare Berry seller (Liechi, Ganlon, Salac, Petaya, Apicot, Lansat, Starf, Enigma, Micle, Custap,
  Jaboca and Rowap Berries, and the Nature Mints, ₽10,000 each), the Solaceon rancher's EV-reducing Berry bundle
  (15 of each for ₽5,000) and Traynee's Power items (part 10).

- Part 11: the Bicycle from Mom (the Cycle Shop gives a Dawn Stone), the Exp. Share from Barry on Route 203, the
  Oval Stone (Oreburgh's guide), Leaf Stone (Eterna Forest's Moss Rock), Shiny Stone (Keira), 10 Potions (Route 201),
  30 Poké Balls (Route 202) and 10 Dive Balls with the Old Rod.

- Part 24: the Lucky Egg, from Mr. Backlot's Happiny once its four Clefairy are freed during Team Galactic's siege
  of the Pokémon Mansion.

Still to come: HM08 from Candice (waits for the owner: it comes with a story Regigigas scene).

## After the Champion

Owner decision 24 (`ProjectStatus.md`):

- Every shop that takes money sells at **half price**. Battle Point shops are unchanged.
- Items **no shop sells** (Nuggets, Pearls, shards, found items) **sell for their full price**. Items a shop sells
  still sell for half, so they can't be bought and resold for a profit.
- The **Youngster at the Survival Area** gives **3 Nuggets** once a day.
- Post-game rematches (Elite Four, Cynthia, Gym Leaders, Battleground trainers) pay **3× prize money**.
