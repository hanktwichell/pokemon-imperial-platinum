The Ultra Beasts aren't static encounters (decision 28, `ProjectStatus.md`). Each is a rare wild Pokémon on its maps once its gate is met: the rate is the share of that map's wild encounters, on the terrain shown. They can be met again after a catch. The Legend Archivist hints at the ones not caught yet. No rare roll happens in a Poké Radar patch, with a partner or in the Safari Game. The Paradox Pokémon come from the Oreburgh Mining Museum's two dealers instead (`PostgameLegendaries.md`).
